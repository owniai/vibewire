# Approach

Interview relentlessly **and** leave a durable paper trail in `.vibewire/`.

ALWAYS on entry to Phase 1:

1. Read [grilling](grilling.md) — design tree, frontier rounds, facts vs decisions.
2. Read [domain-modeling](domain-modeling.md) — inline CONTEXT / ADR updates (and their format files when writing).

Run them together in one session: every interview round may crystallise terms or ADR-worthy decisions; write those under `.vibewire/` the moment they resolve. Do not wait for Synthesize to capture glossary or ADRs.
